The mechanic

Your pet only ever grows.

Growth is just your total entry count — it never goes backward, no matter how long you're away. Mood is separate: purely cosmetic, based on how recently you last wrote, and it never punishes you for a gap.

HAPPY

You wrote today.

0 days since last entry
NEUTRAL

You wrote within the last few days.

1–3 days since last entry
HUNGRY

It's been a while — no judgement, just tap in whenever.

3+ days since last entry
The garden

Every entry plants something.

The background of your diary fills in as you write. What grows depends on how the entry felt — a quiet record of your emotional history that you can literally see.

HAPPY ENTRY

Plants a sunflower.

SAD ENTRY

Plants a patch of grass.

Neutral entries plant nothing — the pet's fine either way, the garden just waits. Nothing is ever pruned; it's meant to get dense.

Privacy

Your entries never leave your device — unless you say so.

  • Local-firstAll entries live in your browser's IndexedDB. The app works fully offline, and that local copy is always the source of truth.
  • No accounts, no serverThere's nothing to sign up for and nothing Ova's author can see. Each person who deploys it runs their own private instance.
  • Backup is opt-in and one-wayCloud storage never overwrites your device — it's a mirror you can restore from, not a second source of truth.

Run your own

No shared server. Every diary is its own instance.

Deploying Ova forks the repo into your own Vercel account, so your build, your data, and your backup are entirely separate from anyone else's.

1

Click Deploy with Vercel below — it forks the repo into your account and deploys it.

2

Open your new app → go to Settings to connect your own encrypted GitHub backup.

Before you connect a backup repo: set it to Private. Entries are encrypted, but a public repo still exposes that you're backing up a diary there.
